Latihan Bahasa Inggris

35 Contoh Soal TKA Bahasa Inggris (Pilihan) SMA/SMK dan Kunci Jawaban

Kategori: TKA SMA Format: Pilihan Ganda Bahasa: Inggris Level: B1–B2

Berikut adalah kumpulan soal TKA SMA pilihan Bahasa Inggris yang dapat digunakan sebagai bahan latihan mandiri. Soal-soal ini mencakup reading comprehension, structure, vocabulary, dan penguasaan grammar yang umumnya diujikan pada tingkat SMA/MA/SMK serta sederajat.

Dalam latihan ini, siswa diharapkan mampu memahami pola soal yang mengacu pada Kurikulum 2013 dan Kurikulum Merdeka. Kumpulan ini juga berfokus pada kemampuan membaca, struktur kalimat, serta penggunaan kosakata sesuai level B1–B2 CEFR.

Soal-soal ini didesain untuk melatih kemampuan siswa dalam menganalisis teks, menemukan informasi utama, memahami makna kata, serta menggunakan struktur bahasa Inggris dengan benar.

Contoh Soal Pilihan Ganda

1
Reading Comprehension
The weather was very cold, so we decided to stay inside and read a book.
  • A. They stayed outside.
  • B. They read a book because it was cold.
  • C. They went swimming.
  • D. They forgot the book.
2
Grammar
If I _____ enough time, I would visit my grandparents this weekend.
  • A. have
  • B. had
  • C. will have
  • D. has
3
Vocabulary
The opposite of "ancient" is ______.
  • A. old
  • B. modern
  • C. historic
  • D. traditional
4
Sentence Structure
My sister _____ to school every day by bicycle.
  • A. go
  • B. goes
  • C. gone
  • D. going
5
Reading
The library is open from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. on weekdays. Students often use it to study before exams.
  • A. The library is closed during the week.
  • B. Students use the library for studying.
  • C. The library opens only during the weekend.
  • D. Students are not allowed to study there.
6
Grammar
She has lived in Jakarta _____ 2018.
  • A. since
  • B. for
  • C. by
  • D. from
7
Vocabulary
A person who writes books is called a ______.
  • A. doctor
  • B. writer
  • C. teacher
  • D. farmer
8
Sentence Completion
We should respect our teachers because they ______ us knowledge and guidance.
  • A. give
  • B. gives
  • C. gave
  • D. giving
9
Reading
Many students are preparing for the national exam. They usually review lessons, do practice questions, and manage their time well.
  • A. Students are not studying seriously.
  • B. They review lessons and do practice questions.
  • C. The exam is held in the evening.
  • D. Students do not need preparation.
10
Grammar
I am interested in learning English because it ______ important for my future career.
  • A. are
  • B. is
  • C. am
  • D. be

Kunci Jawaban

1. B 2. B 3. B 4. B 5. B 6. A 7. B 8. A 9. B 10. B
Catatan: Untuk latihan yang lebih lengkap, siswa dapat menambah jumlah soal dengan variasi teks naratif, descriptive, recount, dan procedure. Fokus utama adalah memahami isi teks, struktur kalimat, serta hubungan antara kata dan makna dalam konteks.
